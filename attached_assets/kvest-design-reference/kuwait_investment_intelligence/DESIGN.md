## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies sovereign-grade fiscal intelligence fused with sovereign-tier predictive artificial intelligence. It serves high-net-worth investors, institutional asset managers, and sovereign wealth analysts engaging with Boursa Kuwait (Premier and Main Markets). The atmosphere must balance institutional gravitas—evoking trust, stability, and fiduciary rigor—with hyper-modern, predictive edge computing.

The aesthetic direction synthesizes **Technical Glassmorphism** with **Futuristic Precision Minimalism**:
- Subdued, layered midnight abyssal surfaces rather than stark absolute blacks.
- High-frequency data precision: razor-sharp 1px borders, luminescence applied strictly to dynamic signals, and micro-metric displays.
- Restrained telemetry: glow and radiance are reserved exclusively for live market movement, AI confidence bounds, and real-time execution states.
- Fully bilingual parity: English and Arabic typographic hierarchies share identical visual density, baseline equilibrium, and structural weight.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep atmospheric baseline with high-impact sovereign accents:

- **Base Canvas (`#080D1F`, `#0D1429`)**: Establishes infinite depth, eliminating eye fatigue across prolonged algorithmic monitoring sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#101831`, `#16213B`)**: Distinct architectural tiers for trading books, AI summary cards, and terminal modules.
- **Electric Flare (`#FF7138` through `#FF925C`)**: High-conviction callouts, primary trading vectors, AI signals, and execution buttons. Gradient usage is strictly linear at 135deg with low stops to prevent consumer-tech softness.
- **AI Neural Blue (`#4F8CFF`)**: Predictive vector nodes, machine learning confidence ribbons, dynamic moving averages, and secondary telemetry.
- **Market Dynamics**:
  - Kuwait Positive / Inflow: `#00E5A3` (Algorithmic Emerald, calibrated against deep navy).
  - Kuwait Negative / Outflow: `#FF3B69` (Crimson Alert).
  - Market Neutral / Halted: `#929DB7` (Atmospheric Slate).
- **Subtle Partitioners**: Borders utilize `rgba(146, 157, 183, 0.16)` on standard edges, escalating to `rgba(79, 140, 255, 0.28)` when containing active AI workloads.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered for mission-critical legibility under dense information loads:

- **Headlines & Executive Metric Hooks**: Set in **Manrope** for authoritative structural clarity with geometric undertones.
- **Narrative, Disclosures, & System Text**: Set in **Inter** to ensure maximum legibility at tiny scale across multi-column market tables.
- **Financial Datasets, Order Books, & Ticker Values**: Set in **JetBrains Mono** with tabular lining figures enabled (`tnum`, `zero`) to ensure perfect vertical alignment across fluctuating KWD price streams.
- **Arabic Script Architecture**: When rendering in RTL for Arabic interfaces, map display and headlines to **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (weights: SemiBold 600, Bold 700) and body layers to **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (Regular 400, Medium 500), matching x-height and optical density to the Latin counterpart.

## Layout & Spacing

The terminal uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for low dead-space utilization:

- **Desktop & Institutional Consoles (1440px+)**: 12-column grid, `margin-desktop` (2.5rem), `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem). Modules lock to standard data clusters: 3-column side inspection, 6-column center algorithmic charting, 3-column AI sentiment and order routing.
- **Tablet & Compact Workstations (768px - 1439px)**: 8-column layout with collapsible trade panels, 1rem gutters.
- **Mobile Handheld (375px - 767px)**: 4-column layout, compacting margins to 1rem. High-density financial rows reduce vertical padding to maintain screen economy.
- **Spatial Grid Rhythm**: An absolute 4px baseline rhythm (`space-xs`: 4px, `space-sm`: 8px, `space-md`: 16px, `space-lg`: 24px, `space-xl`: 40px). Data cells rely on condensed vertical spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize visible rows above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous dark surfaces and delicate structural light framing rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Tier 0 (Root Canvas)**: Solid `#080D1F`. Ground-level data layer.
- **Tier 1 (Base Modules & Panels)**: `#101831` with a fine outline of `rgba(146, 157, 183, 0.16)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Analytics & Filter Shells)**: `#16213B` overlaid with 12px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and semi-translucent alpha (`rgba(22, 33, 59, 0.85)`).
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays, Modals, Popovers)**: `#1B2744` with edge lighting via a directional top border: `1px solid rgba(255, 113, 56, 0.4)`. Shadow is deep and cold: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(79, 140, 255, 0.15)`.
- **AI Focal Glow**: Active AI nodes project a restricted, radiant atmospheric blur: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(79, 140, 255, 0.25)` or `0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 113, 56, 0.3)` depending on neural sentiment weighting.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, micro-radius visual signature:
- Corner rounding is kept compact at `roundedness: 1` (base radius: `0.25rem` / 4px, containers: `0.5rem` / 8px, major cards: `0.75rem` / 12px).
- This tight curvature reinforces mechanical precision, screen economy, and institutional software rigor, preventing the casual consumer-app aesthetic of pillowy rounded corners.
- Pill geometries are restricted solely to status badges, micro market indicators (e.g., Premier Market tags), and numeric ticker delta indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Execution (Buy/Confirm/AI Action)**: Vibrant gradient `#FF7138` to `#FF925C`, crisp `#FFFFFF` text, font weight 600. Minimal border-radius (6px). Hover induces subtle ambient glow (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 113, 56, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Data Filter/Action)**: Glass surface (`rgba(22, 33, 59, 0.7)`), 1px border `rgba(146, 157, 183, 0.24)`, hover state transitions border to `rgba(79, 140, 255, 0.5)` with text shifting to `#F4F7FF`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Text-only using `#929DB7`, switching to `#F4F7FF` with a subtle `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` plate fill on hover.

### Inputs & Financial Selectors
- Background: `#0D1429` with 1px border `rgba(146, 157, 183, 0.16)`. Font: `Inter` for search labels, `JetBrains Mono` for currency and target values.
- Focused State: Border changes to `#4F8CFF`, paired with an inner glow `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px #4F8CFF, 0 0 12px rgba(79, 140, 255, 0.2)`.
- Currency Prefixes: Static `KWD` (د.ك) denomination anchored in `JetBrains Mono` at `#929DB7`.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- Frame: Surface `#101831` enclosed by `rgba(146, 157, 183, 0.16)`. 8px border-radius.
- Header: Monospace status badge, Manrope headline (14px/600), and an optional AI Confidence Meter (a 2px micro-progress bar shifting from `#4F8CFF` to `#FF7138`).

### Data Tables & Order Books
- Headers: JetBrains Mono or Inter `label-caps`, uppercase, `#929DB7`, border-bottom `1px solid rgba(146, 157, 183, 0.12)`.
- Rows: Height fixed to 36px (compact) or 44px (default). Alternating background highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.015)`). Numeric cells right-aligned in Latin, left-aligned in Arabic, strictly rendered in tabular mono figures.

### Status Indicators & Ticker Chips
- Pill containers (`padding: 2px 8px`, `border-radius: 9999px`).
- Inflow/Gain: `#00E5A3` at 12% alpha fill with a solid `#00E5A3` edge and typography.
- Outflow/Loss: `#FF3B69` at 12% alpha fill with a solid `#FF3B69` edge and typography.
- AI Sentiment Signal: `#4F8CFF` pulse badge with a real-time animated center dot indicator.

### Specialized AI Telemetry Node
- A dedicated micro-card displaying autonomous portfolio signals. Features a 1px perimeter border gradient (`#4F8CFF` transitioning to `#FF7138`) with deep `#0D1429` fill and dual-language output toggling dynamically between English and Arabic.
