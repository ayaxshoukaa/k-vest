PRODUCT IDEA
K-vest is a beginner-friendly guided journey that teaches people how to invest in the Kuwait stock market. The user is walked through 4 phases in a fixed order, with a short explainer page before each phase. Nothing is skipped or hidden: the user always knows what's next.

GLOBAL DESIGN
- Very simple and clean: mostly white, one deep-blue accent, big readable text, rounded cards, lots of space.
- Very little text. Use short sentences, icons, and numbers. Never more than 3 short lines on an explainer page.
- Mobile friendly. English and Arabic toggle (RTL for Arabic).
- Subtle animations: fade-ins, smooth page transitions, buttons that gently press down.
- A progress bar at the top once the journey starts: 4 dots (Scan, Research, Practice, ROI) showing where the user is. Users can revisit completed phases.
- Footer on every page: "For education only. Not financial advice."

FLOW
1. Sign In page
2. "Get Started Investing" welcome page
3. Phase 1 intro -> Phase 1: Scan the market
4. Phase 2 intro -> Phase 2: Research with AI
5. Phase 3 intro -> Phase 3: Practice with 1,000 KD
6. Phase 4 intro -> Phase 4: ROI calculator
7. Finish page: short summary of what they did, with buttons to restart or revisit any phase.

PAGE 1: SIGN IN
Centered card with logo, email, password, a big "Sign in" button, plus "Create account" and "Continue as guest". On load, an animated mouse cursor glides to the Sign in button and "clicks" it (button presses with a ripple). Play once, make it skippable, CSS/JS only. Accounts stored in MongoDB with hashed passwords and JWT or session auth.

PAGE 2: GET STARTED
Friendly welcome with the user's name, one headline ("Learn to invest in Kuwait, step by step"), and a simple 4-step illustration of the journey (Scan -> Research -> Practice -> ROI) with one short line each. One big "Get started" button.

EXPLAINER PAGES (one before each phase)
Same layout every time: big icon, phase number and title, 2-3 very short lines explaining what will happen and what they'll learn, then one big button ("Let's go" or "Let's get started"). A small "Back" link too. Example for Phase 1: "Phase 1: Scan the market. You'll see which Kuwaiti stocks are rising, falling, and busiest today. Takes about 2 minutes."

PHASE 1: SCAN THE MARKET
- Top: Boursa Kuwait Premier Market index with today's % change.
- Tabs: Top Gainers, Top Losers, Most Active. Each row: company name, price in KWD, % change (green/red), tiny sparkline, and a simple rating badge (Strong / Neutral / Weak) based on momentum and volume.
- Tap a company for a simple 1-month chart.
- Star icon adds a company to a Watchlist. The user picks at least one company (or taps "Pick for me") before the "Next" button activates. The watchlist carries into Phase 2.
- Data: Oanor API (https://api.oanor.com/kuwait-stock-api/v1/screener and /v1/quote, header x-oanor-key) for lists, and yahoo-finance2 (tickers ending in .KW) for history. Cache results 5 minutes. If loading fails, show a friendly message and sample data. Note: "Prices may be delayed."

PHASE 2: RESEARCH WITH AI
- Chat screen with message bubbles, a typing animation, and 4 suggested question chips (e.g. "Is this company risky?", "What does this company do?", "What is a dividend?", "Should a beginner buy this?").
- A company selector at the top, pre-filled with the user's watchlist. An "Analyze with AI" button gives a short plain-language summary: strengths, risks, simple outlook.
- Backend calls Anthropic (claude-sonnet-4-6). System prompt: "You are a friendly assistant for beginner investors in Kuwait. Answer briefly and simply, in the user's language (English or Arabic). Use the provided market data. Never give personal financial advice; remind users that investing carries risk." Pass the selected company's latest data into the context. API key stays on the backend only.

PHASE 3: PRACTICE WITH 1,000 KD
- Every user gets a virtual 1,000 KD wallet, clearly labeled "Demo - not real money".
- Buy and sell Kuwaiti stocks at current prices (start with the watchlist, allow any company). Show holdings, portfolio value, profit/loss, ROI %, and trade history. Reset button.
- Show a friendly tip card after the first trade ("Good start! Prices change daily, so check back").
- Optional top-10 leaderboard (usernames only, opt-out).
- Save per user in MongoDB. The Phase 4 "Next" button works after at least one demo trade, or via "Skip".

PHASE 4: ROI CALCULATOR
- Simple form: budget in KWD, company (auto-fills current price), buy price, target/sell price, dividends (optional), years held. Offer a "Use my demo results" shortcut that fills the form from Phase 3.
- Result card: profit/loss in KWD, ROI %, annualized return, and a simple Low / Medium / High risk label. Green for profit, red for loss. Counting-up number animation.

FINISH PAGE
Congratulate the user, show a 3-line summary (stocks scanned, demo ROI, calculator result), and offer "Start again" and links to revisit any phase.

TECHNICAL NOTES

- Block skipping ahead via the URL: users can only open phases they have reached.
